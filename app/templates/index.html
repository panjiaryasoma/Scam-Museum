How to read an artifact

The museum does not ask the model to be an oracle.

01 / ML SIGNAL

Statistical risk is only one clue.

The frozen v0.5 classifier contributes a WEAK, ELEVATED, or STRONG text-risk signal. The UI does not present it as a calibrated probability of malicious intent.

02 / EVIDENCE

Artifacts stay observable.

Requests, threats, urgency, suspicious URLs, impersonation cues, and other behaviors are cataloged separately from the ML score.

03 / CONTEXT

Protective language matters.

Warnings such as “never share your OTP” can counter superficial keyword matches when stronger malicious requests are absent.

04 / UNCERTAINTY

Ambiguity is an allowed result.

When a message does not contain enough evidence, Scam Museum can return Insufficient Evidence instead of forcing a binary accusation.

Curatorial statement

A museum for recognizing patterns, not naming villains.

ARCHIVE

Curated public exhibits

The collection uses reconstructed demonstrations inspired by documented scam patterns. It is intentionally separated from private visitor analysis.

PRIVACY

No automatic public display

A message examined in the private room is not automatically added to the gallery. That avoids exposing personal conversations or publishing a false-positive classification.

PURPOSE

Recognition over certainty

The museum teaches visitors to recognize observable manipulation techniques and verify suspicious interactions independently.

PROJECT

Every scam leaves artifacts.

Scam Museum turns the metaphor into the information architecture: collection, exhibits, accession records, an examination room, and curatorial notes.
