# Elevator Pitch

## Primary

**An AI-powered museum that turns suspicious messages into interactive exhibits, exposing scam patterns and manipulation tactics before you become part of the collection.**

## Short version

Scam Museum analyzes suspicious messages with machine learning and deterministic evidence detection, then turns the result into a museum-style exhibit that shows what was actually observed instead of hiding everything behind a single scam score.

## One-line tagline

**Every scam leaves artifacts.**
